RESULTS FROM THE NOTEBOOK

How did the models do?

These figures are saved in the notebook. This website does not train or run the models.

SYNTHETIC SOYBEAN DATA
!

How to read these results: The notebook used 300 synthetic soybean samples across 10 counties and 2018–2022 (192 for training, 48 for validation, and 60 for evaluation). These generated samples are not observed farm or weather records, so the scores describe this experiment only—not how either model would perform on real farms.

YIELD GROUPS · 60 HELD-OUT SAMPLES

Putting yields into 3 groups

Low · Medium · High
Method Correctly grouped Across all groups Telling groups apart
CNN–BiLSTM (images + weather) 78.3% 0.783 0.942
XGBoost baseline 88.3% 0.884 0.955
What do these scores mean?

Correctly grouped is the share of samples put in the right group. The next two scores show whether it did well across all groups and told groups apart. For those scores, closer to 1 is better.

XGBoost classified more of these samples correctly. Real-world performance is unknown.

How should I read this?

The CNN–BiLSTM had a slightly lower average error. XGBoost had fewer large errors and a slightly higher fit score. The notebook does not report the yield unit.

Neither method led on every measure. These are not real yield estimates.

What do these values mean?

Plant greenness: a satellite reading from 0.1 to 0.9. Higher values mean greener plants; this isn't a crop-health diagnosis.

Weather changes: compare each value with what is usual for the area and season. A minus sign means less or cooler; a plus sign means more or warmer. Humidity uses percentage points.

County code: an optional five-digit U.S. county ID. It is saved with your scenario but isn't looked up.
