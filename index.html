What do these scores mean?

Correct answers is the share of examples put in the right group. The next two scores show whether it did well across all groups and told groups apart. For those scores, closer to 1 is better.

What do these values mean?

Plant greenness: a satellite-picture reading from 0.1 to 0.9. Higher means greener in this example; it is not a crop-health diagnosis.

Weather changes: compare each value with what is usual for the area and season. A minus sign means less or cooler; a plus sign means more or warmer. Humidity uses percentage points.

County code: an optional five-digit U.S. county ID. It is saved with your example but is not looked up.
