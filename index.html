UNDER THE HOOD

A hybrid by design.

The notebook combines spatial, temporal, and tabular learning paths, with evaluation and explainability tools.

▦01 / VISION

CNN image encoder

Encodes six aligned Sentinel-2 AG image frames per county-year sample at 224 × 224 pixels.

6 frames · AG imagery
⌁02 / TIME

BiLSTM sequences

Processes a six-frame image sequence and a separate 12-month climate sequence with nine weather features.

12 months · 9 climate features
✳03 / FUSION

Multi-task + baseline

Predicts Low/Medium/High yield-risk tiers and continuous yield; compares full fusion, ablations, and XGBoost.

Classification + regression
